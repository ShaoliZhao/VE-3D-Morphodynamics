# Reading map

The implementation is intentionally smaller than the research systems below. These papers motivate the next model classes to test.

- **moscot** — temporal and spatial mapping with optimal transport; the closest ecosystem reference for transport-based single-cell alignment. Klein et al., *Nature* (2025). https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-024-08453-2
- **GENOT** — neural optimal transport for learning conditional transport maps rather than only finite couplings. Klein et al., arXiv (2023). https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.09254
- **MIOFlow** — manifold-interpolating dynamics for time-resolved single-cell distributions. Huguet et al., *NeurIPS* (2022). https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2022/hash/5cbbeacebde0713f50a6fb0928e43600-Abstract-Conference.html
- **E(n) Equivariant GNNs** — a foundation for rotation- and translation-equivariant learned displacement models. Satorras et al., *ICML* (2021). https://proceedings.mlr.press/v139/satorras21a.html
- **TrajectoryNet** — continuous normalizing flows with trajectory regularization for population dynamics. Tong et al., *ICML* (2020). https://proceedings.mlr.press/v119/tong20a.html
- **Waddington-OT** — optimal transport for developmental trajectories with growth and death. Schiebinger et al., *Cell* (2019). https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2019.01.006
